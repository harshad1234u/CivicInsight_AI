Namma Kavacham AI helps citizens check suspicious messages, links and other possible scams, and look up government schemes against official sources. A few interface strings are still shown in English to Santali and Manipuri users. Earlier machine translations of these strings were not usable, so we are asking qualified speakers to write them. Nothing on this sheet has been translated or reviewed yet.

How to fill in this sheet

  • Write in the script named for your section: Ol Chiki for Santali, Meetei Mayek for Manipuri.
  • Keep the meaning of the English, not its word order.
  • Use plain, natural wording that ordinary citizens understand.
  • Keep product names and identifiers exactly as written (for example Namma Kavacham AI).
  • If the English is ambiguous, write your question next to it instead of guessing.
  • Add a correction or short explanation wherever it helps.
  • If you are unsure of a translation, leave it blank until it is clarified.
  • The key (for example safety.startCheck) is for developers; you do not need to translate it.
